Ha'azinu

הַאֲזִינוּ

Shabbat Shuvah · 19 September 2026 · Deuteronomy 32:1–52

Give ear, O heavens, and I will speak; and let the earth hear the words of my mouth. Deuteronomy 32:1

Moshe asks the sky and the soil to witness. The song is not a lecture. It is a record that outlives the singer. This week the letters are weather: rain, dew, and the small flame that stays after the storm.
