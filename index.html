Digital project · Jewish time and community

נר תמיד

A light for Jewish life, every day.

Ner Tamid is an evolving digital companion for Jewish time, learning, community, and safety. It brings the Hebrew date, candle-lighting times, the weekly Torah portion, prayers, holidays, and practical resources into one calm place.

The central light brightens as Shabbat draws closer, making the rhythms of the Jewish week visible as well as useful.

About the artist

ARG

Sentiment without apology. Color without hesitation.

Anna Rae Gilbert is a Chicagoland-based interdisciplinary artist, educator, writer, and the founder of Anna Rae Art. Her work explores architecture, memory, nostalgia, and the quietly charged narratives that collect inside the places we inhabit.

She studied at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago, earned her undergraduate degree from Herron School of Art and Design, and received her MFA from Maine College of Art. Her practice moves between fine art, public writing, teaching, and small business.
